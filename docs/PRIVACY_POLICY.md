# Privacy Policy for AkAI

**Last updated:** August 29, 2026

AkAI ("the app", "we", "us") is a two-way Filipino Sign Language (FSL)
communication assistant developed by Team 0xACE as an academic capstone project
at Cebu Institute of Technology – University. This Privacy Policy explains how
AkAI handles your information.

## Summary

**AkAI does not collect, store, or share any personal data.** All processing
happens entirely on your device. The app works fully offline and does not send
your information to any server, to us, or to any third party.

## Information We Do NOT Collect

AkAI does **not** collect, transmit, or store:

- Personal identifying information (name, email, phone number, etc.)
- Camera images or video — the camera feed is processed **on your device in real
  time** for sign recognition and is never recorded, saved, or uploaded.
- Microphone audio — speech is transcribed **on your device** for speech-to-text
  and is never recorded, saved, or uploaded.
- Conversation content — messages exist only temporarily in the device's memory
  during an active session and are cleared when the session ends or the app is
  closed. They are never written to permanent storage or sent to any server.
- Location data.
- Usage analytics, advertising identifiers, or tracking data.

## On-Device Processing

All of AkAI's core features run locally on your device:

- **Sign recognition** uses on-device machine-learning models (MediaPipe +
  TensorFlow Lite). The camera is used only for live recognition and no images
  are retained.
- **Speech-to-text** uses an on-device engine (Vosk). The microphone is used only
  for live transcription and no audio is retained.

## Permissions

AkAI requests the following device permissions, used only for the stated purpose
and never to collect data:

- **Camera** — to recognize sign language gestures in real time.
- **Microphone** — to transcribe spoken words to text in real time.
- **Bluetooth / Wi-Fi / Nearby devices** — used *only* for the optional
  "two-device" mode, to send conversation **text** directly between two nearby
  phones over a local, device-to-device connection. This does **not** use the
  internet and no data is sent to any server. The connection carries only the
  recognized text of the conversation; no camera, audio, or personal data is
  transmitted.

## No Internet Transmission

AkAI functions fully offline. It does not require an internet connection to
operate and does not transmit your data over the internet.

## Data Privacy Act Compliance

Consistent with the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173), AkAI is
designed so that no personal data is collected or stored. Conversation text used
in a session is held only in temporary memory and is cleared when the session
ends.

## Children's Privacy

AkAI does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children,
because it does not collect data at all.
